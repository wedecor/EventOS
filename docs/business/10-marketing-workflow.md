## 5. Lead Intake & Handoff to Sales

### Current State

All leads are stored in the **Lead Management Application**.

**Lead source tagging** is done for every enquiry based on where the lead was generated.

Examples of lead sources recorded:

- Instagram Ads
- Organic Instagram
- Website
- WhatsApp
- Referrals
- Repeat customers
- Other sources (where applicable)

Lead source tracking is part of the current lead management process.

#### Lead intake steps (Ilyas)

When a new enquiry arrives, Ilyas:

1. Receives the enquiry from the marketing channel
2. Captures the lead details in the Lead Management Application
3. Tags the lead source (Instagram Ads, website, WhatsApp, referral, etc.)
4. Captures basic customer/event details available at the enquiry stage
5. Assigns the lead to Zakir for further sales follow-up and conversion

Exact qualification fields collected before assignment: see [03-customer-journey.md](./03-customer-journey.md) and [04-sales-process.md](./04-sales-process.md).

#### Assignment notification (Current State)

After Ilyas assigns a lead to Zakir:

- Zakir is notified through the **Lead Management Application notification system**
- Every lead status/change event generates a notification
- When a lead is assigned to Zakir, he receives a notification through the application
- Zakir opens the assigned lead and continues follow-up

The application is the primary source for lead assignment and notifications.

*Cross-reference (from `02-business-model.md`): Ilyas receives every enquiry first, creates lead in Lead Management Application, assigns to Zakir.*

**Not Defined.**

## 7. Marketing Spend & Budget Management

### Current State

#### Instagram Ads spend

- Monthly spend **varies** depending on business requirements and enquiry needs
- Spend changes based on:
  - Number of enquiries required
  - Business workload
  - Event demand
- Ads may be adjusted, paused, or increased depending on workload and performance

There is currently **no fixed budget rule** or formal pause/resume policy.

*Cross-reference (from `02-business-model.md`): approximate historical range ₹4,000–₹5,000/month when active—not treated as a fixed rule here.*

## 9. Event OS Marketing Module Requirements

### Phase 1 Priorities (Founder + Operations)

For Phase 1, the top priorities are:

1. **Lead Source Dashboard**
   - Track where enquiries are coming from (Instagram Ads, organic Instagram, website, WhatsApp, referrals, etc.)
2. **Conversion Funnel by Source**
   - Understand how many leads from each source convert into confirmed bookings
3. **Content Library Linked to Events**
   - Store and organize event photos/videos linked to completed events for future marketing use

Deferred to later phases: detailed ad spend tracking, campaign notes, and advanced marketing analytics.

### Phase 1 (Must Have)

| Capability | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Lead source attribution per enquiry | Future Vision |
| Lead source dashboard (enquiry counts by source) | Future Vision |
| Conversion funnel by source (lead → booking) | Future Vision |
| Content library linked to completed events | Future Vision |
| Campaign/spend tracking (Instagram Ads detail) | Deferred (later phase) |
| Lead intake workflow integration with Lead Management Application | Future Vision |

### Guardrails

- Do not auto-assign leads or auto-respond without human approval (aligned with sales/ops guardrails).
