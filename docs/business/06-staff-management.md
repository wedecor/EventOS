## 1. Executive Summary

We Decor Events runs a **lean permanent workforce** and scales using a **trusted temporary decorator pool**.

| Category | Current Reality |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Permanent decorators** | **2** full-time employees form the core team |
| **Temporary decorators** | Trusted pool engaged on demand; typically **5–6** in busy periods (active pool size: **Not Currently Measured**) |

Staff management is **manual and founder-led**:

- **Zakir** owns day-to-day staffing: manpower planning, selection, attendance/leave negotiation, assignment communication, performance judgement, and on-site coordination.
- **Ilyas** sets governance-level policy and may intervene when required; occasionally provides direct instructions to permanent decorators.

There is **no formal HR system** today—no documented hiring process, onboarding program, training curriculum, performance scorecards, attendance register, or written conduct/safety policies. Training and performance are **practical and observation-based**, delivered through coaching during live events.

**Highest operational risk:** manual coordination, especially:

1. Calling and confirming temporary decorators individually
2. Same-day staff changes and resource reallocation
3. Communicating last-minute changes reliably to every assigned decorator

*For execution context Approved → Completed, see [05-event-execution.md](./05-event-execution.md). For founder roles, see [02-business-model.md](./02-business-model.md) and [01-business-vision.md](./01-business-vision.md).*

## 8. Training & Mentoring

### Tactical Execution (Zakir) — Coaching

- Mistakes corrected immediately during work
- Experienced decorator demonstrates correct approach when needed
- Immediate priorities: decoration quality issues; speed issues threatening completion time
- Specific "non-negotiables" list: **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**

### Tactical Execution (Zakir) — Readiness for Independent Execution

- Determined via practical observation
- Temps: usually assessed within **~5 events** (quality + expected time + minimal supervision)
- No formal "first event alone" process
- No formal downgrade; supervision increases if repeated issues occur (declining quality; poor performance despite coaching)

### Tactical Execution (Zakir) — Staff Selection

| Factor | Applied |
|--------|---------|
| Availability | Yes |
| Individual strengths and experience | Yes |
| Reliability | Yes |
| Quality of previous work | Yes |

**Temporary decorator selection:** Not first-come-first-served. Zakir contacts **preferred decorators first** based on trust, reliability, and past performance.

Clarification (reconciling policy vs execution, no new facts): Zakir contacts **preferred trusted decorators first** based on reliability and previous performance; among those contacted, assignments are confirmed based on **availability and acceptance**.

## 16. Scaling Strategy

### Founder Policy

- Maintain lean permanent team; scale via trusted temporary decorator pool
- No predefined thresholds for hiring 3rd or 4th permanent decorator
- Decorators handle full operational lifecycle today—no dedicated driver, warehouse handler, or operations coordinator roles
- Do not accept bookings if quality or punctuality would be compromised (see SM-17; aligned with [05-event-execution.md](./05-event-execution.md) EX-10)

### Current State — Constraint Without Systems

Operational coordination expected to be first major bottleneck as volume grows without Event OS:

- Staff allocation
- Travel planning
- Scheduling multiple simultaneous events
- Communication across teams

Peak-period pain and failure modes: **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)** where not specified. Frequencies: **Not Currently Measured**.

### Future Vision

Technology before headcount:

- Implement Event OS to automate scheduling and staff coordination
- Multiple warehouses across Bangalore
- Geographic proximity-based assignment to reduce travel
- Performance metrics and ratings to improve assignment recommendations
- Continue lean permanent workforce with trusted temporary decorator pool for demand fluctuations

### Not Defined

- Event volume triggers for 3rd/4th permanent decorator
- Target permanent team size at 30+ events/month
- Whether dedicated non-decorator operational roles will be introduced

## 17. Event OS Staff Module Requirements

*Founder vision for internal systems. Business policy is defined in Sections 1–16; this section records what founders want systems to support.*

### Phase 1 (Must Have)

| Priority | Capability |
|----------|------------|
| 1 | **Staff Master** — permanent and temporary profiles: classification, contact, skills/experience, event history, employment status, compensation type, performance metrics, overall rating |

**Principle:** Support decision-making. Do not replace founder or operations judgement.
