## 4. Founder-Validated Master Pain List

### Top 5 Current Business Pain Points (Priority Order)

1. **Dependency on Zakir for operations**
   - Most event execution knowledge, coordination, vendor handling, and decision-making depends on Zakir
2. **Lack of profitability visibility per event**
   - No clear view of revenue, actual costs, and profit for each event
3. **Manual tracking across different areas**
   - Customer payments, vendor expenses, inventory movement, and operational details managed through memory, WhatsApp, and informal methods
4. **Inventory and material management challenges**
   - No structured tracking of reusable items, availability, movement, damages, and maintenance
5. **Scaling operations while maintaining quality**
   - Increasing event volume requires better SOPs, checklists, and process visibility for consistent execution

### Cross-reference — Domain doc pains (not duplicated here)

| Master pain | Related domain references |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Zakir dependency | `05` Top pain #1; `06` coordination risks; `04` key-person memory |
| Profitability visibility | `02`, `09` event P&L not measured |
| Manual tracking | `03` fragmented data; `09` informal finance; `07` informal vendor records |
| Inventory challenges | `08` no structured tracking; `05` distributed storage |
| Scaling + quality | `05` Must Fix Before 30+; `13` SOP gaps |

### Future Vision

Event OS Phase 1 modules should directly address pains #1–#4; pain #5 addressed via SOPs + checklists (`13`) integrated in Event OS.

### Event OS Phase 1 — #1 Priority Pain to Reduce

**Dependency on Zakir for operations**

Phase 1 should prioritize reducing single-person operational dependency through:

- Centralized event workspace and operations visibility
- Structured assignment, procurement, and communication records
- Checklists and status tracking that do not require Zakir's memory

(Other pains remain important but are secondary to Phase 1 primary focus.)

### Acceptable Now vs Painful Today (Current Volume ~15–20 events/month)

#### Acceptable now

- **Inventory and material management challenges** — current volume managed through experience and manual coordination
- **Some manual tracking across areas** — still manageable at current event volume

#### Painful today

- **Dependency on Zakir for operations** — bottleneck and risk as volume increases
- **Lack of profitability visibility per event** — difficult to understand margins and make better decisions
- **Scaling operations while maintaining quality** — increasingly difficult as event count grows
