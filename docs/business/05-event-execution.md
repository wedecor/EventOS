## Executive Summary

We Decor has **no formal Sales-to-Operations handoff** because **Zakir owns both sales and operations**. When advance is verified, the lead moves to **Approved** and Zakir immediately begins execution planning—mentally, via WhatsApp, and in disconnected applications. **There is no operations playbook, checklist, or centralized calendar today.**

**Typical flow:** Plan soon after Approved → procure materials closer to event (fresh flowers 1 day before or event day) → assign staff as event approaches → coordinate logistics manually → reconfirm customer 1–2 days before → execute on event day → visual quality check → photos (best effort) → Zakir marks **Completed**.

**Team:** 2 permanent decorators + additional manpower (trusted temporary decorator pool). Assignment manual, experience-driven. **No max events/day policy** — decline if quality or punctuality would be compromised.

**Top execution pains:** Zakir as single coordinator, same-day multi-event logistics across Bangalore, no ops calendar, WhatsApp-only coordination, no Approved→ops plan linkage.

**Capacity:** 15–20 events/month typical, ~25 peak, **30+ target**. Scaling requires **Event OS first**, then staff and warehouse — not surge pricing.

**AI policy (operations, future):** Assist planning, conflicts, checklists, travel, change impact, incident summary — **never** automated staff notifications without human review; **never** replace Zakir's operational judgement.

**Event OS Phase 1:** Operations Workspace at Approved, ops calendar, staff assignment, material checklist, customer coordination tasks, execution checklist, logistics conflict detection, on-time tracking.

*For customer-facing pre/event/post experience, see `03-customer-journey.md`. For advance and booking confirmation, see `04-sales-process.md`.*

## 1. Event Execution Philosophy & Sales Handoff

### Current State

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Formal handoff | **None** — Zakir owns sales + operations |
| Trigger | Advance verified → **Approved** → planning begins immediately |
| Ilyas post-Approved | Primary role ends; may assist ad hoc — not standard |
| Checklists / playbook | **None** |
| Planning channel | Memory, WhatsApp, Lead Management Application (status), Quotation and Billing Application |

### Operational Flow (Confirmed)

```
Customer pays advance
    → Zakir verifies payment
    → Lead status: Approved
    → Zakir reviews event details (type, date, location, design, timing, requirements)
    → Mental execution plan (materials, reuse, purchase, team, transport, timing)
    → Materials arranged closer to event date
    → Staff assigned based on availability, complexity, same-day load
    → Logistics coordinated
    → Customer reconfirmed near event date
    → Event day execution
    → Completed
```

### Success Criteria (Internal — Before Completed)

- Decoration completed **before** agreed completion time  
- Matches agreed design direction within scope, budget, feasibility  
- All agreed items installed  
- No unresolved operational issues before team leaves  
- Customer received agreed decoration  
- Photos captured when possible  
- Venue left in acceptable condition  

### Non-Negotiables

- Late arrival from poor planning  
- Significantly different decoration than agreed  
- Leaving site incomplete  
- On-site unkeepable promises  
- Unprofessional behaviour with customer  
- Quality sacrificed for speed  
- Unresolved issues at departure  

### Punctuality

**No fixed buffer policy** (no "arrive X hours before"). Planning case-by-case using complexity, size, travel, traffic, same-day load, completion time. **Expectation always:** complete before agreed time.

### Quality vs Speed

Never compromise customer commitments or quality. Plan properly to avoid conflicts. Recovery tactics (split team, etc.) — **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### Completed Status

**Zakir** marks Completed after work finished. Timing — **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### North Star Connection

Operational target = deliver as promised, on time. "Customer leaves with a smile" assessed **informally** (appreciation, WhatsApp, repeat, referrals, reviews). **No operational quality score today.**

### Future Vision

At **Approved**, Event OS auto-creates **Operations Workspace**: linked customer/quote, design, material checklist, procurement tasks, staff assignment, logistics plan, event timeline, customer tasks, execution checklist.

## 5. Transportation & Logistics

### Vehicles

Tempo/goods vehicle or suitable transport by volume. **No dedicated fleet.** Driver: Zakir, team member, or hired — detail **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

Transport cost **included in bundled quotation**; higher for distant/outside Bangalore.

### Planning

After staff, materials, timing confirmed — **no fixed schedule.** Routes manual: timing, location, traffic, same-day load. Traffic considered via **experience** — **no formal buffer**. Route prioritization — **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### On-Site Delivery

Typical load: backdrops, artificial flowers, props, fabric, lights, balloons, fresh flowers, tools. Partial prep before transport; final assembly at venue.

**Common challenges:** parking, apartment security, lifts, stairs, access restrictions. Zakir coordinates access pre-event; team on-site if needed.

### Geographic Scope

- **Primary:** Bangalore — all areas if feasible  
- **Outside Bangalore:** case-by-case (size, distance, viability, feasibility)  
- **Distant locations:** earlier departure, extra travel time  

### Logistics Failures (Observed)

Traffic, vehicle delays, shortages, access delays, wrong address, need second trip — recovery **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### Future Vision

Event OS: travel time between same-day events, traffic estimates, conflict detection, impossible schedule flags, team/vehicle optimization alerts — **human decides**.

## 6. Event Day Execution Workflow

### Before Leaving

- No fixed assembly location  
- Informal material check — no checklist  
- Design reference: WhatsApp, customer photos, past event photos  
- Morning-of reconfirmation when required (location, timing, access)  

### Travel

Team together or separately. Zakir with team or direct to venue. No formal travel protocol — **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### Arrival & Setup

- Access via customer or venue representative  
- Setup location confirmed before work  
- **No standardized install sequence** — typical: backdrop → fabric/structure → flowers → balloons → lights → finishing  
- Setup duration — **Not Currently Measured**  
- Quality: visual verification by Zakir or responsible team member — no checklist  

### Completion & Departure

Complete when agreed decoration installed, adjustments done, ready before agreed time. Customer walkthrough — sometimes, not standard. Photos usually post-setup, pre-celebration. Team leaves when work done and issues addressed.

### Post-Setup Same Day

Balance payment: **Zakir via WhatsApp** — not ops team. No formal debrief. **Completed** marked by Zakir after successful finish — exact timing **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### Same-Day Second Event

Pack, load, travel, repeat. Turnaround depends on distance, traffic, complexity — **no standard target**. Coordination detail — **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

## 13. Event OS Operations Module

### Founder Vision

Every **Approved** booking automatically becomes a **complete operational workspace** — single record for planning, materials, staff, logistics, execution, photos, incidents, completion — replacing WhatsApp/memory/verbal coordination.

**Principle:** Deliver promised decoration on time with consistent quality; reduce operational stress; **preserve human judgement**.

### Phase 1 MVP (Go-Live Priority)

| Priority | Capability |
|----------|------------|
| 1 | Operations Workspace auto-created at Approved |
| 2 | Centralized Operations Calendar (daily/weekly, same-day view) |
| 3 | Staff assignment linked to each event |
| 4 | Material checklist + procurement tasks |
| 5 | Customer coordination tasks (reconfirmation, access, timing) |
| 6 | Execution checklist (pre-departure, on-site, completion) |
| 7 | Logistics planning + travel time + schedule conflict detection |
| 8 | On-time completion tracking |

### Phase 2

Change management workflow, incident log, photo gallery linked to event, WhatsApp communication timeline, advanced analytics, AI operational recommendations.

### Module Boundaries

| Module | Starts | Owns |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Sales** | Lead → Approved | Discovery, quote, negotiation, advance |
| **Operations** | Approved → Completed | Planning, procurement, staff, logistics, execution, completion |
| **Calendar** | Approved | Scheduling, conflict detection, timeline |
| **Staff** | Approved | Assignment, availability, workload |
| **Finance** | Approved onward | Advance, balance, payment records |
| **Customer Intelligence** | Full lifecycle | History, preferences, milestones, photos |

**At Approved:** Sales complete → Operations Workspace created → Calendar entry → planning begins → Customer Intelligence timeline continues.

### AI for Operations (Future)

| Capability | Policy |
|------------|--------|
| Staff assignment recommendations | Yes — Future |
| Same-day conflict warnings | Yes — Future |
| Material checklist from design | Yes — Future |
| Travel time / route optimization | Yes — Future |
| On-site change impact assessment | Yes — Future |
| Incident summary from WhatsApp | Yes — Future |
| Automated staff notifications | **No — human review required** |

**Principle:** AI assists planning; **never replaces** Zakir's operational judgement.

### Success Criteria

**Qualitative:** Every Approved → organized plan; nothing on Zakir's memory; team knows where/when/what; same-day manageable; issues visible early; consistent quality as team grows; less coordination stress.

**Measurable (future):** Higher on-time rate, fewer conflicts/mistakes, faster assignment, better utilization, less WhatsApp dependency — baselines **Not Currently Measured**.
