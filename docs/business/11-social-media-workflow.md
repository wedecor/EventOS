## Purpose of This Document

This document describes **how We Decor manages organic social media today** (Current State) and what We Decor **wants to become** (Future Vision), specifically:

- Platforms managed (primarily Instagram)
- Content sourcing (event photos/videos, design references)
- Posting workflow (who decides, who posts, timing)
- Customer permission and privacy handling for public posts
- Engagement workflow (DMs, comments, enquiries from organic posts)
- Content organization and reuse for sales conversations
- Event OS content library and social workflow requirements

It is derived from direct founder interviews (Ilyas) and tactical execution interviews (Zakir). **Founder business policy** is distinguished from **tactical execution (Zakir)**. Metrics not tracked are marked **Not Currently Measured**—never estimated. Undefined decisions are marked **Not Defined**.

**Intended audience:** Founders, product managers, engineers, AI agents, investors, and business leaders building Event OS Content Library and social/marketing integrations.

**Source:** Founder interview (Ilyas) + Tactical interview (Zakir), July 2026–July 2027

## 1. Executive Summary

### Current State

Organic social is **Ilyas-led**, decor-focused:

- Platforms: **Instagram** (primary), **Facebook** (maintained)
- Ilyas selects and publishes organic posts/reels; Zakir may supply event media
- Posting is event-driven; no fixed schedule; frequency **Not Currently Measured**
- Content: decor setup/final output only; no customer/people photos published
- Permission: informal (WhatsApp/verbal) when needed; no formal consent record
- Organic Instagram DMs: Ilyas intake → Lead Management Application → tag organic Instagram → assign Zakir
- Storage: Google Drive folders; no structured event-linked library today

### Founder Policy

**Not Defined** (formal posting cadence, consent workflow if policy expands beyond decor-only).

### Future Vision

Phase 1: **Content Library Linked to Events** (decor photos/videos only). Consent workflow deferred given current practice.

## 5. Posting Workflow (Organic)

### Current State

- **Ilyas** is responsible for content strategy, selecting content, and managing organic social posts/reels
- **Publishing** is also handled by Ilyas
- **Zakir** may provide event photos/videos or operational inputs
- Overall organic social media responsibility remains with **Ilyas**

#### Posting frequency (Current State)

- Instagram organic posting frequency is **not fixed**
- Posts and reels are published based on available content, especially after events when photos/videos are available
- There is currently **no defined posting schedule** or fixed frequency
- Posting frequency: **Not Currently Measured**

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

## 6. Customer Permission & Privacy

### Current State

Customer permission for posting event photos/videos is handled **informally**:

- Permission is usually taken through customer communication (WhatsApp or verbal confirmation)
- There is **no formal consent record** or documented approval workflow today

Structured consent capture: **Not Currently Implemented**.

#### Current content practice (decor-only)

- We primarily capture photos/videos of the **decoration setup and final decor output**
- We do **not** capture or publish pictures of customers or people
- Therefore, a customer photo consent workflow is **not required** for the current content process

*Cross-reference (from `05-event-execution.md` PH-04): Instagram permission informal; case-by-case.*

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

## 7. Engagement (DMs, Comments, Enquiries)

### Current State

#### Organic Instagram DM enquiries

1. Enquiry received through Instagram DM
2. **Ilyas** handles initial response and captures lead details
3. Lead moved into Lead Management Application
4. Lead source tagged as **organic Instagram**
5. Lead assigned to **Zakir** for further sales follow-up

Process follows the same lead management flow as other enquiry channels (see [10-marketing-workflow.md](./10-marketing-workflow.md)).

Comment handling and other engagement workflows: **Not Yet Interviewed (Zakir)**.

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

## 8. Content Storage & Reuse

### Current State

Event photos and videos are stored in **Google Drive**:

- Photos/videos are uploaded and stored in Drive
- Content is organized based on available folders and event references

There is currently **no formal content management system** or structured content library linked to events.

### Future Vision

Align with Phase 1 **Content Library Linked to Events** from [10-marketing-workflow.md](./10-marketing-workflow.md).

## 9. Event OS Social/Content Requirements

### Phase 1 Priorities (Founder + Operations)

Phase 1 content tracking should focus on **decoration photos/videos only**.

Phase 1 requirement:

- **Content Library Linked to Events** (aligned with [10-marketing-workflow.md](./10-marketing-workflow.md))
- Store decor photos/videos linked to the respective event for future marketing use

Customer photo consent workflow: **not required for Phase 1** given current decor-only content practice.

Deferred: permission/consent workflow enhancements (if content policy expands beyond decor-only in future).

### Phase 1 (Must Have)

| Capability | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Content library linked to completed events (decor photos/videos) | Future Vision (aligned with Doc 10) |
| Permission/privacy flag per event/media set | Deferred (not required for decor-only Phase 1) |
| Tags/metadata for portfolio search (event type, theme, colours) | Future Vision |

### Guardrails

- Do not auto-publish to social platforms without human approval.
