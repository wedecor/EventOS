## Purpose of This Document

## 1. Executive Summary

### Current State

Customer support is **informal and channel-led**:

- **Zakir** owns execution/event support after handoff; **Ilyas** for tech/marketing/major cases
- Channels: **WhatsApp** (primary), phone; post-booking not mainly Instagram DM
- Complaints: Zakir reviews → on-site fix if possible → coordinate team/vendors → case-by-case compensation; no ticketing
- Escalation to Ilyas: tech/marketing/major relationship decisions; no fixed matrix
- Google reviews: sent via Lead Management Application WhatsApp feature after completion; timing/not-every-event **Not Defined**; status not tracked
- Post-completion issues: extremely rare; no separate workflow

### Founder Policy

Formal complaint/compensation policy and review request cadence: **Not Defined**.

### Future Vision

Phase 1 support focus:

1. Customer communication timeline (per event)
2. Complaint/issue notes (per event)
3. Review request tracking

Full ticketing/escalation workflow deferred.

## 3. Support Channels & Ownership

### Current State

Customer support ownership is split based on issue type:

#### Zakir (primary for execution-related support)

- Primary owner for event-related, execution, and operational issues
- Handles customer concerns related to designs, setup, changes, and event delivery
- Main owner for customer support **after the lead is handed over for execution**

#### Ilyas (select cases)

- Handles certain cases related to technology, website, lead management, or marketing-related customer interactions when required

#### Support channels (Current State)

- **WhatsApp** (primary channel)
- **Phone calls**

Customers mainly use WhatsApp for communication, updates, and issue reporting.

Instagram DMs may be used for initial enquiries, but post-booking support is mainly handled through **WhatsApp and calls**.

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

## 4. Complaint & Issue Handling

### Current State

Current complaint handling workflow:

1. Customer raises the issue through WhatsApp or phone
2. **Zakir** reviews and understands the issue
3. If the issue is during event execution, the team tries to **resolve immediately at the venue**
4. If required, Zakir coordinates with team/vendors to fix the issue
5. Compensation, adjustment, or further discussion handled **based on the situation**

There is currently **no formal complaint ticketing** or escalation workflow.

*Cross-reference (from `03-customer-journey.md`): Formal complaint handling process — none documented.*

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

---

## 5. Escalation Model

### Current State

Issues are escalated to **Ilyas** based on type and impact.

Typical cases where Ilyas may get involved:

- Technology-related issues (website, applications, lead management systems)
- Marketing-related concerns
- Major customer concerns requiring founder-level discussion
- Situations where business decisions or customer relationship decisions are needed

Operational issues related to event execution are generally handled by **Zakir**.

There is currently **no fixed escalation matrix**; major escalation criteria are handled case-by-case.

*Cross-reference (from `05-event-execution.md` IR-01): Zakir handles issues; Ilyas exceptional only.*

## 8. Post-Event Support Boundaries

### Current State

Post-event support issues are **extremely rare**.

Current experience:

- No significant customer complaints after event completion documented as a pattern
- In one instance, lights stopped working — handled as an immediate issue

There is currently **no separate post-completion support workflow** because such cases have not occurred regularly.

*Cross-reference (from `03-customer-journey.md` CJ-23): Customer journey ends after balance collected and review request sent — no further follow-up today.*

### Future Vision

**Not Defined.**

## 9. Event OS Customer Support Requirements

### Phase 1 Priorities (Founder + Operations)

For Phase 1, the top priorities are:

1. **Customer Communication Timeline**
   - Maintain history of important customer conversations, updates, and decisions linked to the event
2. **Complaint/Issue Notes Linked to Event**
   - Capture customer issues, actions taken, and resolution notes against the specific event
3. **Review Request Tracking**
   - Track whether a Google review request was sent after event completion

Full complaint ticketing and escalation workflow: **deferred** (customer issues currently very rare).

### Phase 1 (Must Have)

| Capability | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Customer communication timeline linked to event | Future Vision |
| Complaint/issue notes linked to event | Future Vision |
| Google review request status per event/customer | Future Vision |
| Full complaint ticketing workflow | Deferred (later phase) |
| Issue resolution notes and escalation record | Partial (notes in Phase 1; formal escalation workflow deferred) |

### Guardrails

- Do not auto-send review requests or support messages without human approval.
